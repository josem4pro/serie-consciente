Installation requirements

What the manufacturer requires.

Before putting it up for sale, we asked Technogym for the installation requirements, so you know from the start whether your space is suitable. These are the figures and diagrams their installation specialist gave us.

01

Wall anchoring

  • Any type of wall will do, as long as each anchor point can take a 50 kg pull-out load.
  • The fixings are chosen to suit the wall material, always guaranteeing that minimum load.
  • It is anchored with two crossbars, at 200.3 cm and 17.5 cm from the floor. Without its covers, the frame is 126 cm wide and 201 cm high.
02

Training space

  • You need 2 metres of clear space in front of the machine to exercise safely.
  • The training area is 200 × 190 cm.
03

Built into a recess

  • It can fit in a recess at least 215 cm high, 180 cm wide and 33.5 cm deep.
  • Installed this way, the top and rear covers are not fitted.

Source: Technogym Iberia, installation department, 1 October 2026.

Questions

Before you decide.

Can I see it working?

Yes. It is in Las Caletillas (Candelaria) and you can try it by appointment. Message us on WhatsApp and we will arrange a day and time.

Is it in working order?

Yes. Technogym's official technical service checked it in October 2025, and on 23 September 2026 it was filmed switched on and working. We will show you the report at the viewing.

Is there technical service in Tenerife?

Yes. Technogym has an authorised technical service in Tenerife.

Is transport included?

No. The price includes dismantling and installation at the destination; transport is paid by the buyer, because the cost depends on the area of the island and the access to the site. We put you in touch with the Technogym team that handles removals; to give a quote, they usually ask for a video of the access and of the destination wall.

How much space does it need?

A vertical wall of at least 170 × 230 cm and about 200 cm of clear space in front for training. Technogym accepts any type of wall as long as each anchor point can take a 50 kg pull-out load; the fixings are chosen to suit the wall. It can also be built into a recess 180 × 215 cm and 33.5 cm deep, without the top and rear covers. When closed, it sticks out about 45 cm from the wall. A standard earthed socket is all it needs.

Do you issue an invoice?

You receive a bill of sale with the unit's serial number.

Can I reserve it?

As it is a single unit, priority goes to whoever views it and reserves it first.
